
PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026
TIPZ ("We," "Us," or "Our") operates the TIPZ mobile application and web platform (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

To provide a functional and social environment, we collect the following types of information:
• Account Data: When you register, we collect your email address, username, and password.
• User-Generated Content: We store the public text, stock tickers, timestamps, and thread data that you post to the network.
• Transaction Information: If you send or receive a tip, financial transactions are processed securely through our third-party payment gateway (e.g., Stripe). We do not store your raw credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers. We only retain transaction histories (amounts, timestamps, sender/receiver IDs) to display in your profile dashboard.
• Device & Usage Data: We may collect basic diagnostic data, such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, and application crash logs to improve app stability.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected data for the following purposes:
• To create and maintain your public profile and user "Karma" tracking metrics.
• To facilitate peer-to-peer tipping transactions securely.
• To monitor the network for fraudulent activity, pump-and-dump schemes, or behavior violating our Terms of Service.
• To provide customer support and fix application bugs.

3. DATA SHARING & DISCLOSURE

• Public Information: Your username, profile details, public posts, and accuracy track-records are visible to all users on the platform.
• Third-Party Service Providers: We share necessary data with trusted third parties to run the app (such as authentication providers, cloud hosting servers, and financial transaction processors). These parties are strictly obligated not to disclose or use your information for any other purpose.
• Legal Requirements: We may disclose your personal data if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or the SEC).

4. DATA SECURITY & RETENTION

The security of your data is important to us. We utilize industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your information both in transit and at rest. We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through the app settings or by contacting support.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Service is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old (or the legal age to trade securities in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13.
